Unwinator privacy policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Unwinator does not collect, transmit, sell, share or remotely store any personal information, browsing history or webpage content.

Unwinator processes the visible text of webpages locally within the user's browser for the sole purpose of transforming that text into Unwinese and spoonerised text.

Webpage content is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party. The developer has no access to the webpages processed by the extension.

Unwinator stores only the user's extension preferences, such as whether the extension is enabled and the selected mangling intensity. These settings are stored using Chrome's extension storage.

Unwinator does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or telemetry and does not send data to external servers.

No user data is sold or transferred to third parties.

Unwinator's use of information is limited solely to providing its user-facing text-transformation function and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
